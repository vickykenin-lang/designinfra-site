Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Designinfra ("the app", "we") accesses, uses, and protects information when you connect your Google Account to authorise the app to manage your YouTube channel.

Who this app is for

Designinfra is a personal-use tool built and operated by its developer to automate the publishing of YouTube Shorts to a single, authorised YouTube channel. It is not a public product and is not offered to the general public for sign-up.

Information we access

When you authorise Designinfra through Google Sign-In, the app requests the following Google API scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — allows the app to upload and manage videos on the authorised YouTube channel only. The app does not read, modify, or delete any other content on your channel beyond what is required to publish videos.

We do not access your email content, contacts, files, payment information, or any other Google service beyond the scope listed above.

How we use this information

Access is used solely to upload video files, prepared in advance by the channel owner, to the authorised YouTube channel. No data obtained through this access is used for advertising, profiling, resale, or any purpose unrelated to publishing videos on the authorised channel.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data obtained through this authorisation with third parties. Access tokens are stored securely and used only by the app itself to communicate directly with the YouTube Data API.

Data retention

OAuth tokens are retained only for as long as necessary to operate the app and are deleted if authorisation is revoked. You can revoke access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

Data security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect any credentials and tokens associated with this app from unauthorised access, alteration, or disclosure.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
