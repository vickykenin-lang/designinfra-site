Designinfra

A personal YouTube Shorts automation tool.

What this app does

Designinfra is a personal-use automation tool that uploads short-form videos (YouTube Shorts) to a single, authorised YouTube channel on behalf of its owner. It uses the YouTube Data API to publish pre-produced video files directly to the connected channel, removing the need for manual uploads.

How it works

  • The app connects to one YouTube channel via Google OAuth, authorised directly by the channel owner.
  • It requests only the permission needed to manage and upload videos to that channel.
  • No content, credentials, or channel data is shared with any third party.
  • The app is used solely by its developer for personal content publishing workflows.
